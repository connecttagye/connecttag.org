Application: Yemen View (يمن فيو) · Package ID: com.telecom.yemenview · Effective Date: September 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

Yemen View is a free mobile utility and telecommunication guide developed by Connect Tag. The app is designed to help users in Yemen easily browse and activate telecom services, packages, USSD codes, offers, and emergency numbers.

At Connect Tag, protecting your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the handling of information and device permissions in compliance with Google Play Developer policies. By using Yemen View, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

💡 Important Guarantee: Yemen View does NOT collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal identifiable information, passwords, or personal call records to external servers.

2. Local Data Storage (Offline-First)

Yemen View is built using an Offline-First architecture. All essential data is cached locally on your device:

  • App Preferences: Selected language (Arabic/English), theme mode (Dark/Light/System), and custom UI interactions.
  • Telecom Database: A locally stored database of services, bundle prices, activation codes, and emergency numbers to ensure uninterrupted offline functionality.
  • Favorites: Your bookmarked services for quick offline access.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

The app only requests the minimum necessary permissions required for its functionality:

Permission Purpose & Functional Need
INTERNET Used solely to check for bundle updates, new promos, and to synchronize the local database when an internet connection is available.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Checks whether an active network connection exists before making API requests, optimizing battery and resource usage.
ACCESS_WIFI_STATE Checks Wi-Fi connectivity status.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Optional on Android 13+) Used to notify users about important telecom updates or critical service notices. You can disable this anytime in your system settings.
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID Anonymous Google Advertising ID for serving compliant, non-intrusive ads to keep the app free.

4. Third-Party Services

The application integrates trusted third-party SDKs to provide updates, analytics, and advertisements in compliance with Google Play Developer policies:

  • Google Play Services: Powers in-app updates and in-app review flows.
  • Google AdMob: Displays contextual advertisements to support the free operation of the app.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): Manages user consent for personalized ads and privacy standards.

5. Children's Privacy

Our services do not target individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a minor has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate action.
